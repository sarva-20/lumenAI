Detailed Overview of Solar Energy Subsidies and Financial Assistance in Tamil Nadu
1️⃣ Central Government Subsidy – PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana

Objective:
The PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana aims to promote rooftop solar installation in residential households across India. It offers direct financial subsidies and enables users to generate their own electricity and feed excess power to the grid.

Eligibility:

Residential/domestic users only.

Must have a grid-tied rooftop solar system.

Must be installed by an MNRE (Ministry of New and Renewable Energy) approved/empanelled vendor.

Subsidy Amounts (as applicable in Tamil Nadu):

System Capacity	Approximate Subsidy	Notes
1 kW	₹30,000	Covers ~60% of installation cost
2 kW	₹60,000	Covers ~60% of installation cost
3 kW or more	₹78,000 (maximum)	For systems ≥3 kW

(Source: Green Pages India, DTNext, The New Indian Express)

How to Apply:

Visit the National Rooftop Solar Portal: https://pmsuryaghar.gov.in

Register using your electricity consumer number and details.

Choose an empanelled vendor listed for Tamil Nadu.

Get a quotation and approval from the local DISCOM (TANGEDCO/TNPDCL).

After installation, submit photos and documents for inspection.

Subsidy is transferred directly to your bank account within 15 days once verified (TNPDCL confirmation – DTNext).

State Progress:

Tamil Nadu has targeted 25 lakh households to adopt solar rooftops under this scheme (The New Indian Express).

In the Coimbatore division, the subsidy rate is 60% for up to 2 kW and 40% for additional capacity up to 3 kW.

Key Benefit:

Reduces electricity bills drastically; users can get up to 300 free units per month depending on usage and production.

Documents Required:

Recent electricity bill

ID proof (Aadhaar, PAN)

Roof ownership proof (Property tax or EB bill)

Bank account details for subsidy credit

Implementation Agency:
Tamil Nadu Power Distribution Corporation Limited (TNPDCL) under MNRE.

2️⃣ State Government Incentive – Chief Minister’s Solar Rooftop Capital Incentive Scheme (Tamil Nadu)

Objective:
To encourage more households in Tamil Nadu to install solar rooftops and complement the central subsidy, making solar energy accessible for middle- and lower-income families.

Subsidy Details:

₹20,000 per kW capital incentive from the Tamil Nadu State Government.

Applicable for grid-connected domestic solar PV systems.

Can be availed in addition to the central subsidy.

Eligibility Criteria:

Must have a valid domestic electricity connection from TANGEDCO.

The system must be installed by an approved vendor as per TN government guidelines.

Preference for 1 kW to 3 kW rooftop systems.

Application Process:

Apply through the TANGEDCO/TN Energy Development Agency (TEDA) website.

Submit technical specifications and vendor details.

Post-installation inspection by TANGEDCO officials.

Subsidy disbursed upon successful verification.

(Source: environment.tn.gov.in, Govt Schemes India)

Example:
If a user installs a 2 kW rooftop solar system:

Central subsidy: ₹60,000

State subsidy: ₹40,000 (₹20,000 × 2 kW)

Total benefit: ₹1,00,000 on installation cost (approx ₹1.6–₹1.8 L system).

3️⃣ Other Incentives and Tariff Reductions
For MSMEs (Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises)

Tamil Nadu has implemented a 50% reduction in rooftop solar network charges for MSME industries.

Encourages small factories and workshops to adopt solar and cut grid dependency.
(Source: SolarQuarter, 2023)

For Government Buildings

Tamil Nadu government has initiated a 40 MW rooftop solar tender to power government offices and reduce energy costs.

Part of the state’s broader renewable energy transition plan.
(Source: The New Indian Express, Oct 2025)

💰 Financial Help / Banking Loan Options
1. State Bank of India (SBI) – Surya Ghar Solar Loan

Dedicated loan product under PM Surya Ghar Scheme.

Loan amounts: up to ₹2 lakh for small residential systems; up to ₹6 lakh for larger systems.

Interest Rate:

Up to ₹2 lakh → ~6.00% p.a.

₹2 – 6 lakh → ~8.15% p.a.

Margin Money (user contribution): 10% of project cost.

Tenure: up to 10 years.

No collateral required for loans ≤ ₹2 lakh.

Loan directly linked with national rooftop portal for subsidy integration. (Source: SBI, The Economic Times)

2. Union Bank of India

Offers Digital Solar Loans for residential customers.

No income proof required for loan up to ₹2 lakh in certain cases.

Quick approval with vendor quotation and KYC documents.

Scheme highlighted during PM Surya Ghar awareness drives in Madurai (Lotus Times, 2025).

3. Other Banks & NBFCs

Indian Bank, Canara Bank, and IREDA also offer similar green loans.

EMI ranges between ₹1,200 – ₹2,000/month for a 1–2 kW system after subsidy.

Example Calculation:

2 kW rooftop system cost = ₹1,60,000

Less central subsidy (₹60,000) + state subsidy (₹40,000) → Net cost ₹60,000

Loan for ₹60,000 @ 6% p.a. for 10 years → EMI ≈ ₹666/month.

🧾 Required Documents for Subsidy & Loan

Latest electricity bill (TANGEDCO connection)

Proof of property ownership / NOC from building owner

Aadhaar + PAN

Passport-size photo

Bank account details (for subsidy transfer)

Vendor quotation and technical design

⚙️ Implementation & Processing

Register on https://pmsuryaghar.gov.in.

Choose approved vendor (empanelled under MNRE/TEDA).

Installation & inspection by local DISCOM.

Submit final claim on portal.

Subsidy credited within 15 days (as per TNPDCL announcement).

Monitor production and grid export via mobile app or portal.

🟩 Conclusion

Tamil Nadu residents can save significantly by combining central and state subsidies, and accessing low-interest solar loans. For most 2–3 kW systems, over 50–60% of installation cost is covered. With quick processing by TNPDCL and national portal integration, solar adoption has become one of the most financially viable green investments in Tamil Nadu today.
