CENTRAL (NATIONAL) SCHEMES
1) PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana (National Rooftop Solar Programme)

Short description: Central household rooftop solar subsidy & program (national rooftop portal / DISCOM aggregation model). Designed to accelerate residential rooftop adoption and integrate with net-metering; includes central financial assistance (CFA) and utility aggregation models. 
pmsuryaghar.gov.in
+1

Eligibility: Residential/domestic consumers with grid-tied rooftop installations; must use empanelled/approved vendors and follow MNRE technical specs. 
Ministry of New and Renewable Energy

Subsidy / typical amounts: Typical CFA figures published on the national portal: ₹30,000 per kW for 1 kW, ₹60,000 for 2 kW, and up to ₹78,000 cap for ~3 kW (operational guidelines vary by category and utility). Some descriptions say up to ~40% of system cost or specific slabbed CFA — check portal for your DISCOM. 
pmsuryaghar.gov.in
+1

View more (official): National Portal / PM Surya Ghar — pmsuryaghar.gov.in / MNRE grid-connected rooftop page. 
pmsuryaghar.gov.in
+1

Documents required (typical): Latest electricity bill (consumer number), Aadhaar, PAN, bank account details (for subsidy credit), property/roof proof, vendor quotation, signed application. 
pmsuryaghar.gov.in

Where to apply: Register & apply on https://pmsuryaghar.gov.in
 or through your local DISCOM’s rooftop solar portal (link on national portal). 
solarrooftop.pmsuryaghar.gov.in

2) MNRE — Grid-Connected Rooftop Solar Programme (operational guidelines & CFA)

Short description: MNRE’s overall program (operational guidelines, CFA mechanisms, RESCO/utility led models and net-metering guidelines). It’s the policy/technical backbone for rooftop subsidy rollouts. 
Ministry of New and Renewable Energy

Eligibility & notes: Residential and non-residential categories defined in guidelines; utilities and aggregators have dedicated operational routes. Refer to MNRE for the exact category definitions & timelines. 
Ministry of New and Renewable Energy

View more: MNRE scheme page & operational guideline PDFs. 
Ministry of New and Renewable Energy

Where to apply / documents: As per MNRE & national portal; DISCOM channels for local processing. 
Ministry of New and Renewable Energy

3) PM-KUSUM (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Urja Suraksha evam Utthaan Mahabhiyan)

Short description: Central scheme for farmers — supports (A) decentralized ground/stilt solar plants, (B) standalone solar agriculture pumps, (C) solarisation of existing grid-connected pumps. Designed to replace diesel pumps and boost agri-solar. 
Ministry of New and Renewable Energy
+1

Eligibility: Farmers, farmer groups, panchayats or agro-entrepreneurs as per state implementation rules; differing components have different eligibility. 
Ministry of New and Renewable Energy

Subsidy / support: Central financial assistance up to ~30% (typical) for pumps; states often add top-ups. Component-specific caps and percentages vary — see the MNRE PM-KUSUM page. 
Ministry of New and Renewable Energy

View more: MNRE PM-KUSUM page. 
Ministry of New and Renewable Energy

Documents required / where to apply: Farmer ID (Aadhaar), electricity connection details, land/pump ownership proof, bank details — apply via state implementing agency / DISCOM / TEDA (state portal). 
Ministry of New and Renewable Energy

4) Chief Minister’s Solar Rooftop Capital Incentive Scheme (Tamil Nadu)

Short description: Tamil Nadu state capital incentive that stacks with central subsidy to make residential rooftop installs cheaper.

Eligibility: Domestic (residential) consumers with TANGEDCO/TNPDCL connection; system must be grid-connected and installed by empanelled/approved vendors.

5) TEDA / TANGEDCO / TNPDCL — Empanelment, Net-metering & Fast-track processing

Short description: TEDA and Tamil Nadu DISCOMs maintain empanelled vendor lists, technical specifications, and process net-metering + subsidy verification. TNPDCL has announced fast processing timelines (subsidy credit in ~15 days if docs/vendor details OK — local news/utility advisory). 
tnebltd.gov.in

View more / vendor lists: TEDA / TNEB / DISCOM vendor PDFs and the national portal vendor list (state filter).

tnebltd.gov.in

6) MSME rooftop network charge concessions (TN)

Short description: Tamil Nadu has policies (notifications/news) that reduce rooftop network charges for MSMEs (reported 50% reduction in some announcements) to encourage industry uptake. Check DISCOM orders for precise eligibility and usage caps.
